Proposal A, round 3: sections and lids

This is the arrows spine, split into sections. Each section starts under an opaque lid that shows only its name and job. Lids open as the story reaches them: first KV and cbft, then scorch as a whole, then (nested inside scorch) the introducer, the persister and the merger, and finally the disk. Each lid covers exactly the area of what it hides, so opening one never moves anything. The arrows between sections stay visible, so even closed boxes show data flowing through them.

Try it: click the steps, or press ↓/↑. Section headings in the story column mark where a new lid opens. A chart dims and drops its leader line while what it measures is still under a lid, or out of view.

Notes

  • Lids vs. growing boxes. Lids keep geometry fixed, which you asked for. The cost is that the closed overview has big, mostly empty boxes. The other way is to grow boxes as they open, like an accordion. That reads as more “zooming in,” but it shifts everything below.
  • Drill-down depth. Two levels here: sections (KV, cbft, scorch, disk) and the scorch triplet. A third level could open the persister’s step 3 into its four workers, or the merger’s step 2 into the actual tier staircase.
  • Re-closing. Scrolling back up closes lids again, so each step always looks the same however you got there. The scenario chapters would start with everything open.
  • Simulation. When a lid is closed, its box could still show a summary state, such as a busy dot or a “blocked” tint, so the black box stays alive while its insides are hidden.
